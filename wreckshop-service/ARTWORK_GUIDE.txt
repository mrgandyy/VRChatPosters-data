WRECKSHOP WORLDS — ARTWORK GUIDE

POSTERS
Standard: slots 1–8. Premium: slots 1–32.
Create portrait artwork at 2:3, recommended 1024 x 1536 pixels.
Keep important lettering inside a 5% safe margin. Use bold readable type; small text loses clarity in-world. Upload individual posters, not an atlas. Other aspect ratios are fitted with padding, not cropped. Do not pre-stretch images.

UPLOAD
Use /group mine to find your group ID.
/poster submit group:<ID> slot:17 image:<file>
Premium groups can use slot numbers through 32 with the same command.
/poster batch accepts up to eight images, each with its own slot. Repeat for additional slots. Eight per batch is not your total allowance.
Each submission goes through review. Approval enters the publishing queue; it does not appear instantly in every client. Select your group's code in the updated world to see its artwork.
Use /poster list to check submissions. /poster remove restores the default for a slot after publication.

PREMIUM EXTRAS
Entrance billboard: 4:1, ideally 2048 x 512; /billboard submit.
Photo-wall backdrop: 4:3, ideally 2048 x 1536; /backdrop submit.
Set your official VRChat group page with /group page for the entrance join link.

FILES
Static PNG, JPEG or WebP. Maximum 12 MB and 30 megapixels. Both dimensions at least 256 and no greater than 8192 pixels. No animated images. Export RGB artwork with a deliberate background. Use artwork you are authorized to share and follow the server and VRChat rules.

COMPATIBILITY
Slots 17–32 require the updated world with the second poster sheet. Existing slots 1–16 remain compatible with older worlds. Group codes select artwork; they do not grant staff, dancer or VIP permissions.
